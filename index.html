Performance guide
Pad bank BPick a genre. It switches on the next bar (or beat/4 bars, see quantize) and the tempo glides over. When stopped, a genre pad starts the set. Press the current genre again to end on the next bar.
Pads 1-5Kick, Drums, Bass, Chords, Melody on/off. Instant.
Pad 6 · BuildHold to build tension: snare roll speeds up, riser, the low end filters away, kick and bass drop out. Release and it drops on the next downbeat with an impact and every layer back in.
Pad 7 · StutterHold to repeat the current slice. Hit soft = 1/4 note, medium = 1/8, hard = 1/16.
Pad 8 · MutateNew progression and melody at the next quantize point.
Lower keysWhite keys play the 7 chords of the current key (I to vii). The chord stays until you pick another. Black keys: C# back to the auto progression, D# key up a step, F# key down a fifth, G# key up a fifth, A# major/minor flip.
Upper keysLead, locked to the scale: white keys walk the scale, black keys play the nearest chord tone. There are no wrong notes. The auto melody steps back for 2 bars while you play.
KnobsK1 DJ filter (left = low-pass, right = high-pass, center = off) · K2 Energy · K3 Density · K4 Chaos · K5 Space · K6 Echo · K7 Tempo · K8 Gate. Density, Tempo and Gate are relative to the genre (center = genre default), so switching genre never jumps a knob.
JoystickX bends the pitch, Y adds vibrato.
AutopilotPlays the arrangement for you (intro, build, drop, break, drop 2, outro), including builds and drops. Your pads still override it.
ComputerSpace start/end · Shift+Space stop now · Esc panic · 1-8 pads (hold 6/7) · Shift+1-8 genres · Z X C V B N M chords I-vii · A auto chords · Q W E R T Y U I O P lead.
MappingClick Setup controller once: it asks you to turn each knob and hit each pad, then remembers what your MPK sends (works with pads in note, CC or PROG CHANGE mode). The MIDI monitor shows what each message is mapped to. MIDI Learn re-maps a single control. If your lowest key isn't C3, click Split and press the key where the lead zone should start.
